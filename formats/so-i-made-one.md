# So I made one (founder story)

**Family key:** `founder` (proven)

## Structure
- **Hook slide:** a white-box hook in two parts (`hook_box`): the gap you could not fill at the top, "so I made one" near the bottom.
- **Later slides:** bare photos with no text at all. The product, the making of it, the product in use.
- **Caption:** the story in your own words, ending with where to find it.

## Why it works
A true gap, a real maker, and nothing hidden in the slides. The hook carries the story; the bare photos let the product speak.

## Fill-in slots
- `hook_box[0]`: what you looked for and could not find ("I could not find ... for my ...").
- `hook_box[1]`: so I made one.
- Later slides: `["section", "", ""]`, using product sections in the order you want shown.
- Caption: why you made it, who it is for, one real detail only you would know.

## Notes
Only your own story. Do not invent customers, quotes or reactions. Do not claim results the product cannot back.
