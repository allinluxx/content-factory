# What they need

**Family key:** `needs` (proven)

## Structure
- **Hook slide:** "What they actually need to {start something}".
- **Slides 2 to 5:** each a real need, written to remove pressure. Heading states the need; body says why it is smaller or simpler than people fear.
- **Last slide:** permission. A calm line that tells the viewer they are already enough.

## Why it works
It answers the worried question under the real question: am I doing enough? Relief earns saves and shares.

## Fill-in slots
- `{start something}`: the thing your audience is nervous to begin.
- 4 to 5 needs: simple, true, and mostly free. One of them may point gently at the product.

## Notes
Never shame the viewer or imply they are failing. If one need is the product, say so plainly; do not hide an advert in the list.
