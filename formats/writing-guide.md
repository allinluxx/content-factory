# Writing guide

How every post should sound, whatever the niche.

## Voice
- Warm and first person. One person talking to one person.
- Specific beats general: "one wonky carrot" beats "fresh produce".
- Plain words, short lines. Read it aloud; if you would not say it, change it.

## Slides
- A short heading (a few words) with 2 to 4 lines underneath.
- A slide with no heading and no body is a bare photo, used on purpose.
- One idea per slide.

## Captions
- Start with an emotional title line, then a long caption: what you feel, what happened, what you would ask the reader.
- End with a question or an invitation, then the hashtags from the brand's tagset.

## Batches
- Proven formats make up most of a batch. New angles are marked `"test": true` and kept to a minority.
- Lead with your strongest proven formats; hold seasonal posts to the right moment.

## Truth and trust
- No invented anecdotes about real people. If a story is not yours, do not tell it as if it were.
- No claims the brand cannot back up. Check `never_say` in brand.json.
- Affiliate, gifted or sponsored posts say so clearly in the caption (for example "ad", "gifted" or "affiliate link"), near the start.
- Only use photos of people who agreed. Follow `people_rules` in brand.json.

## Borrowing
- Patterns (a hook shape, a list structure, an emotion) are fair to reuse.
- Other creators' wording and images are not. Rewrite from the pattern, never from the post.
