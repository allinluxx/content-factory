# Traditions list

**Family key:** `traditions` (proven)

## Structure
- **Hook slide:** "6 things I plan on doing with my {people} every {season or occasion}".
- **Slides 2 to 6 or 7:** one tradition each. Heading is the activity in a few words; body is one concrete detail (what, where, one small rule).
- **Last slide:** an invitation to add to the list.

## Why it works
It reads like a plan, not an advert. People save it and send it to someone, which is the strongest signal this format earns.

## Fill-in slots
- `{people}`: the viewer's own people (kids, grandchildren, friends).
- `{season or occasion}`: spring, birthdays, Sundays, a first week at home.
- 5 to 6 traditions: cheap, doable, specific. Each should be something the brand's product can sit next to, never something it has to be forced into.

## Notes
Phrase as intention ("I plan on") so it is true for you and easy for others to copy. Caption asks: what would you add?
