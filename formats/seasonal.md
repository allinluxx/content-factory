# Seasonal

**Family key:** `seasonal` (listed in `posting.seasonal_families`)

## Structure
- **Hook slide:** a list or a mood tied to a time of year ("6 cosy things to ... this winter").
- **Slides 2 to 6:** one idea each, short heading, 1 to 2 lines.
- **Photos:** the hook photo must be from that season (`need.season`).

## Why it works
People look for ideas just before a season starts. It is timely, specific and easy to act on today.

## Fill-in slots
- `need.season`: the season or occasion exactly as it is written in hook_tags.json.
- The idea list: practical and small.

## Notes
The planner holds seasonal posts to the later part of a batch (`posting.seasonal_from`) and saves photos of that season for them. Plan batches so the post goes out shortly before the season. Add a new family to `posting.seasonal_families` for each extra season.
