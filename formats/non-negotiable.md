# Non negotiable

**Family key:** `nonneg` (proven)

## Structure
- **Hook slide:** "Non negotiable as a {identity} {doing something that matters}". One line, on a hook photo showing that person.
- **Slides 2 to 6:** one non-negotiable each. Short heading (a command or a promise), 2 to 4 lines underneath saying why.
- **Last slide:** the softest, most human one. A product slide is welcome here if it truly fits.

## Why it works
Viewers see themselves in the identity and swipe to check whether they agree. Each slide is a small opinion, so people comment with their own.

## Fill-in slots
- `{identity}`: who the viewer is (a {mum}, a gardener, a first-time dog owner).
- `{doing}`: the part of life the list is about.
- 5 non-negotiables: each a belief you really hold and can stand behind.
- Photos: the hook photo shows a person who matches the identity; later slides use section photos or one `family` photo.

## Notes
Keep each belief gentle and honest. No claims you cannot back up. Mark the post `"test": true` if the identity is new.
